## Meaning over bits

WaSeCom studies what happens when the transmission target is not an exact bit sequence but the task-relevant meaning consumed by a large AI model. Wireless channels fluctuate, source distributions shift, and edge resources remain constrained; average-case training is not enough.

I contributed to modelling and experimental work for large-model inference under changing channels, including distributionally robust training simulations and evaluation in low-bandwidth settings. The resulting work appeared in the _IEEE Journal on Selected Areas in Communications_ in 2026.
