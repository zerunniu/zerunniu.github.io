## Research needs an interface

The DUAL Group website makes members, publications, and projects easy to discover without creating a maintenance burden for the lab. I owned the design, information architecture, implementation, deployment, and ongoing updates.

The project demonstrates a different part of research engineering: translating complex work into a clear public system, balancing visual identity with accessibility, and building a contribution workflow that a research group can sustain.
